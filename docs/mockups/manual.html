Quick Start · one page

Test a router's IPTV quality in 5 steps

Use this page once everything is installed. Each step has one action.

1
Connect
Sender PC to the router's WAN port. Controller and Receiver PC to LAN ports. Tablets on the router's Wi-Fi and on USB to the Receiver PC.
2
Open ZeroLoss
On the Controller PC. Devices are found automatically. Check that every device shows ✔.
3
Choose
Test type, Video quality (for example UHD · 3840×2160 · 35 Mbps) and Duration.
4
Press Start
Wait until the button turns red and says Stop.
5
Read the result
When the test ends, press Show report. Green boxes = no problem, red = packets lost.

Do not change router settings or use the router's Wi-Fi from other phones during a test. It changes the result.

Chapter 1

Connect the cables

Every PC connects to the router under test. Only the Controller PC also connects to the internet.

1
Connect the Sender PC to the router's WAN port.
✔ You should see: the router's WAN light turns on.
✖ If not: try another cable. See Troubleshooting → "Sender PC not found".
2
Connect the Controller PC and the Receiver PC to LAN ports.
✔ You should see: both PCs get an address from the router (for example 192.168.1.x).
3
Connect each tablet to the Receiver PC with a USB cable.
✔ You should see: the tablet starts charging. Wi-Fi is set up by ZeroLoss later.
✖ If the tablet asks "Allow USB debugging?", tap Allow. This happens only the first time.

Chapter 7

Read the results

Every box is 5 minutes for one receiver.

✔GreenNo problem in these 5 minutes.
⚠YellowNo packets lost, but a warning (for example jitter higher than usual).
✖RedPackets were lost or jitter was over 150 ms. The router is the likely cause.
◆PurpleA problem, but not caused by the router (for example the tablet dropped packets itself).
○GreyNo data (a device was disconnected).

Overall result

✔ PASSRan the full time. No red, purple or grey boxes.
✖ FAILAt least one red box.
◐ INCOMPLETEStopped early, or some boxes are purple or grey. No red so far.

In a FAIL report, press Show where it failed at the top. It jumps to the first red box and opens the 1-second detail.

Glossary

Words used in ZeroLoss

Video quality
The video sent to receivers: name, video resolution and bitrate. Example: UHD · 3840×2160 · 35 Mbps.
Video resolution
Width × height of the sent video. Example: 3840×2160.
Screen size, Monitor
Your PC monitor. Not related to the test result.
Router
The router under test (called DUT in technical report sections).
Sender, Receiver
The PC that sends the video, and the PC or tablet that receives it.
Jitter, Loss
How uneven packet arrival is (ms), and how many packets never arrived. The two most important numbers.
